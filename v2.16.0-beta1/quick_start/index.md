# Quick Start

Source: https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/

Supernotify can be installed and used without writing any YAML. These pages take about ten minutes:

1. [Installation](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/installation/index.md)
1. [Send your first notification](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/first_notification/index.md)
1. [What to do next](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/next_steps/index.md) - starting with an automation and a dashboard
1. [Basic concepts](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/basic_concepts/index.md) - the main ideas and terminology explained

There are also [advanced concepts](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/advanced_concepts/index.md) plus lots of [recipes](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/recipes/index.md) to try out.

If you don't end up liking it, then use the [removal](https://supernotify.rhizomatics.org.uk/latest/quick_start/removing/index.md) guidance to clean it up, and if you can, [leave a note](https://github.com/rhizomatics/supernotify/discussions/new/choose) on what didn't work for you.
